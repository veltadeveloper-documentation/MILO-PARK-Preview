I skate when the city sleeps.

Port Meridian, after dark. The ledges cool down, security changes shifts, and the marble gets fast. I've been chasing that window for fourteen years — camera bag on one shoulder, spare board under the other arm, and a strict no-daylight policy (allergies, probably).

This page is the personal archive: every tape worth keeping, every spot worth the push, and the boards that carried me there. Start anywhere. The map's the good part.
